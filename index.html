Before you donate

General eligibility guidelines

Standard baseline criteria used broadly across blood donation programs. Always confirm with the collecting hospital or blood bank on the day.

01

Age & weight

Generally 18–65 years old and at least 50 kg body weight.

02

Gap between donations

At least 3–4 months since your last whole blood donation.

03

General health

Feeling well on the day, no fever, and adequate sleep the night before.

04

Eat & hydrate

Have a meal and drink water before donating — avoid donating on an empty stomach.
